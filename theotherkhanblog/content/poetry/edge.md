The shape of ﬁre, where <br> 
a ﬂicker abandons the ember.<br>

Dirt at the border, the last person\
killed in a war.<br>

The way the ﬁrst letter of your\
name sounds when someone speaks it.<br>

The curve where the brown\
of your eyes turns to black.<br>

When a child glances\
upon the vulnerability of their parents.<br>

The year that they forgive them.<br>

The moment when a moth realizes death is imminent.<br>

The bottom plank of your coffin nestling into the ground.<br>

The ﬁnal drop of water from ablution trickling down your body.<br>
 
The crevice of a cracked mirror, jagged edges turning soft,<br>
finally meeting my own gaze.
