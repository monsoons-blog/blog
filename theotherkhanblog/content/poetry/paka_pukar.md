Pakar aur pukaar\
In do lafzon ke beech ka farq?<br> 
Ek Alif.

Yeh mere liye aasan nahin.

_Pakar:_\
Rooh ke band kamre\
Jeena aahon ke darmiyan\
Chhupna, chhupaana, chhup jaana…\
Zauq-e-tishnagi — hamesha, hamesha, hamesha\
Nazdik aana<br>

_Pukaar:_\
Sawaal poochna, beshumaar sawaal…\
Har chiz mukhtalif, magar\
Lamhon ka raabta\
Kashmakash
 
_Pakar:_\
Khule haath, khaali haath…\
Apne dil ki dehleez par sona\
Tafreeq\
Khud se anjan rehna<br>
 
_Pukaar:_\
Iss mitti mein hogi humari mulaqat<br> 
And backwards we’ll go to khaak.\
Hadd ab anhad ho jaegi\
Aur khaak dur tak paunch jaegi\
I will become a Judas tree in Bursa\
You a beetroot plant in Ooty!\
Sirf mitti aur mitti kaafi<br>
 
_Pukaar_ matlab<br>
main yaahan khadi hoon, tum wahaan khade ho\
main nahin hiloongi, tum nahin hiloge\
Na main apne aap ko chhota karungi\
Na tum apne aap ko chhota karoge<br>

Here is an invite\
Give me your hand\
Mine is open
